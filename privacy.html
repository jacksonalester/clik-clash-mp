Privacy Policy and Terms of Use · Last updated November 13, 2026 · World Class Creative Design and Tech LLC

The short version

You can play without an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location. The game has no ads and no tracking or analytics tools.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (level, coins, unlocked items, stats, daily challenges and settings) and the callsign you type are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device. Clearing your browser's site data, or using Settings > Reset progress, deletes it.

What is sent when you play online

If you choose ONLINE, your device connects to our game server. While you are connected the server receives your gameplay inputs (movement, aim and fire) so it can run the match and show you to other players. As with any internet service, the server and its hosting provider can see your IP address while you are connected. We do not keep match data or connection logs tied to you after you leave, beyond the short-lived technical logs our hosting provider keeps to run the service.

Hosting

The game files are served by GitHub Pages and the game server runs on Railway. These providers process standard web request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the service, under their own privacy policies.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It contains cartoon violence.

Purchases

This version has no real-money purchases. Coins are earned by playing and cannot be bought, sold, transferred or cashed out. Cosmetic items are for use in the game only and have no cash value.
