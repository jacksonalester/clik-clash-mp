THE FINE PRINT

Privacy

Your progress (coins, unlocks, stats, settings) is saved only on this device. No account is needed and we do not collect personal information, show ads or use tracking.

If you choose ONLINE, your live gameplay inputs (movement, aim and fire) are sent to our game server so other players can see you in the match. Like any website, the server can see your network address while you are connected. Nothing from online play is stored after you leave.

Terms

Play fair and be kind. Cosmetic items are for in-game use only and have no cash value. Coins are earned by playing and cannot be bought or cashed out. The game is provided as is.

SUPPORT

Game froze or looks wrong

Close the tab and open the game again. Your progress is saved after every match and every purchase.

My progress is gone

Progress is stored in this browser on this device. A different browser, a private window or clearing site data starts a fresh save.

Online will not connect

Check your connection and try again in a minute. The live arena restarts briefly when we ship an update.

Controls feel off

Settings has left-handed controls, aim assist, screen shake and a low graphics mode for older phones.
